Synopsis

A mysterious invite lures nine creators—director, SFX artist, stuntman, AD, DP, sound tech, and a 'perfect' couple—to an all-night "competition." The studio doors seal. A cold Voice lays out the rules: film a terrifying scene by deadline, hit the viral threshold, or someone dies. As their shorts blend into real carnage, egos fracture and alliances form; scripts are weaponized; film gear becomes lethal. Each day's prompt escalates the mayhem until only a Final contestant remains to face a last, impossible choice—and the truth about who's been watching.
